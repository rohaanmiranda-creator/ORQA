BOTTLE WEIGHT
The empty bottle's own weight, measured by you. The pod adds it to the water it measures to report gross weight. This was hardcoded to 180 g, which is why an "empty" capture always read exactly 180 — it was the constant coming back, not your bottle.
CELL POWER
On battery, power save powers the cell down between weighs. A woken cell needs a moment for the bridge to settle before its reading means anything — hold it awake for a measurement session. Calibration holds it automatically.
